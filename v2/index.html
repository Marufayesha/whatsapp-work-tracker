📡 Every chat, unopened

v1 could only see the chat on your screen. v2 reads all recent chats in the background, including new messages that arrive while you're busy elsewhere.

⚡ Catches up instantly

On first start it reads the last few days of every chat. After that, each new message is processed the moment it arrives.

🍎 macOS-style design

Traffic lights, translucent glass and a segmented toolbar. To-do looks like Reminders, Messages like the Messages app.

🌓 Light, Dark & text size

Auto, Light or Dark appearance and four text sizes. It looks native on Windows, macOS and Linux.

🔔 Smarter follow-ups

Four groups: They replied, Waiting on them, They will send, and I promised. Promises like “kal bhej dunga” are tracked too.

🔒 Read-only by design

It never sends a message, never marks chats as read (no blue ticks) and never opens a chat. Tracking is blocked and everything stays on your computer.
