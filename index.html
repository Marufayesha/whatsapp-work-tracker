WhatsApp Work Tracker

Your WhatsApp is full of work — invoices, payments, “kal tak bhej dena”. This Chrome extension quietly turns those messages into tasks, follow-ups, payments and reminders. No server, no AI bills, 100% offline.

📥 Smart Inbox

Work messages are detected automatically. Sort each one with a single tap: Task, Follow-up, Details or “Not work”.

📅 Understands dates

“kal tak”, “20 tak”, “Monday ko”, “EOD” → real due dates, counted from the day the message was sent.

🔔 Follow-ups both ways

Tracks what others owe you and what you owe them. Reminders, snooze, and it notices when the reply arrives.

🗂️ Details vault

GSTIN, PAN, IFSC, UTR, amounts and documents are pulled out and saved in one searchable place. Click to copy.

🏢 Company tags

Handles several businesses? Every task is auto-tagged to the right company.

📊 Daily Sweep + Report

Checks every chat active today, read or unread, so nothing gets missed. Export to Excel/CSV.

🧾 Compliance calendar

GST, TDS, ITR and ROC due dates appear on the schedule next to your own tasks.

🔒 Private by design

It only reads, never sends or clicks on its own. OTPs aren't stored, passwords are redacted and Aadhaar is masked. No cloud.
